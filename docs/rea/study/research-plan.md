# REA Study — fiscal research plan

Review EC 132/2023, LC 214/2025 **and subsequent amendments**, official Receita Federal/Comitê Gestor materials and published technical notes. Record article, effective date, source URL, retrieval date, interpretation, uncertainty and reviewer sign-off per rule. No normative rate is preapproved in this package.

Investigation topics: taxable events, operation classification, applicable bases, rate components, transition, exceptions, rounding, effective dates, amendments, versioning and legal uncertainty. Any unresolved topic must block production-grade fiscal claims.
